# Fleshed-out Concept

### 1) Core Concept: The Sensory Gap”

The game is built on a fundamental asymmetry:

- **The Living Player (The Survivor):** Has full physical agency but impaired perception due to deep-sea hypoxia. Vision is desaturated (grayscale/sepia). They cannot distinguish color-coded puzzles (wires, lights, chemicals), but can move objects, use tools, and operate the submarine’s machinery.
- **The Ghost Player (The Echo):** Has total perception via high-contrast vision. Surfaces are translucent, and critical items glow in vivid colors (Red = Danger, Blue = Interaction, Gold = Collectible). They cannot speak or directly touch physical objects without spending **Energy**.

---

### 2) Communication & Interaction Mechanics

Because the Ghost cannot talk, players develop a non-verbal language using VR-specific inputs:

- **Haptic Tapping:** The Ghost taps surfaces. The Living player feels directional haptic feedback and hears 3D spatial audio (for example, three taps on the left means look left).
- **Manifestation Meter:** The Ghost spends energy to briefly flicker lights, frost glass to write symbols, or move small objects.
- **Spirit Tagging:** The Ghost tags an object to make it glow faintly for the Living player for a few seconds.
- **The Tether:** The Ghost is tethered to the Living player to maintain tension. They can only move a limited distance unless they find **Spectral Anchors**.

---

### 3) The Mechanical Skeleton (Progression)

Instead of plot-specific acts, the game progresses through escalating mechanical complexity:

| **Phase** | **Mechanical focus** | **Player interaction** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase I: Calibration** | Basic movement and trust | Simple look here, do that” puzzles. Ghost leads Living to hidden codes. |
| **Phase II: Expansion** | Asymmetry and specialized zones | Introduce Fixed Entry” areas. Ghost enters vents and hazards to clear paths for Living. |
| **Phase III: Convergence** | High-pressure coordination | Rapid-fire puzzles. Both players act simultaneously in different locations to prevent failure. |

---

### 4) Puzzle Design: Individual vs. Collaborative

#### Individual puzzles (solo agency)

These ensure players are not always waiting on each other and each role has solo wins.

- **For the Living (manual and tactile):**
    - **Sequence Overload:** Pull heavy, rusted levers in a specific order. Physically scrub grime off metal plates to reveal symbols.
    - **Tactile Circuit Board:** Fix a panel by tracing wires based on **texture** (Smooth, Braided, Corrugated) rather than color.
- **For the Ghost (ethereal and observational):**
    - **Ethereal Conduit:** Navigate a 3D spirit maze inside walls or pipes to reach a power node. The Living player cannot see this maze at all.
    - **Spatial Alignment:** Move to a specific point in 3D space so floating **Spirit Shards** align into a 2D shape or code.

#### Collaborative puzzles (the asymmetric core)

- **Color-Coded Repair:** Ghost signals which colored wire to cut or which colored valve to turn, because the Living player sees them as the same shade.
- **Overwatch Navigation:** Ghost guides the Living player through a darkened maze filled with physical traps the Living cannot see.

---

### 5) Leveling System: Mementos

The relationship evolves through exploration and small narrative rewards.

1. **Discovery:** The Living player finds **Mementos** (personal items) hidden in the environment.
2. **The Anchor:** Returning them to the Ghost anchors the Echo to the world, leveling up powers.
3. **Upgrades:**
    - **Level 1:** Ability to point and hand-track (visible as a faint mist).
    - **Level 2:** **Possession**: briefly flip physical switches.
    - **Level 3:** **Echo Sight**: show the Living player a brief visual replay of past events in a room.
